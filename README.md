# Advanced SQL Sales & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

In this project I have analyzed sales and customer data using SQL to uncover
business trends, customer behavior, and sales performance.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective is to analyze:

- Sales performance over time
- Customer growth and behavior
- Revenue trends
- Product performance
- Customer contribution to sales
- Running and cumulative sales
- Year-over-year growth
- Key business opportunities

## 🔍 Business Questions

1. How have sales changed over time?
2. How many customers were acquired each year?
3. What is the running total of sales?
4. What is the year-over-year sales growth?
5. Which products generate the most revenue?
6. Which customers contribute the most revenue?
7. What are the best-performing months?
8. Which categories are growing or declining?
9. Who are the top customers?
